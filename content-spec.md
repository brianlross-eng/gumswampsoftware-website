## 2. Content Tone & Style

### 2.1 Voice
- **Professional but approachable** - Technical expertise without pretension
- **Confident but humble** - We know our capabilities without overpromising
- **Community-focused** - Emphasis on open-source collaboration

### 2.2 Writing Guidelines
- Use active voice where possible
- Keep sentences clear and concise
- Avoid jargon unless explaining it
- Use American English spelling
- Include project status clearly (Not Ready for Release, Active Development)

### 2.3 Tone Examples

**Good:**
> "Lazarus helps you bring broken packages back to life."

**Avoid:**
> "Lazarus is a utility designed for the purpose of resurrecting malfunctioning software modules."
