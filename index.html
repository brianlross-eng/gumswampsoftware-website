About Gum Swamp Software

Gum Swamp Software is an independent software company based in the coastal lowlands of South Carolina. We build open-source tools and generate sustainable revenue through software products, donations, and licensing.

Our Mission

"To create sustainable open-source software that solves real-world problems while maintaining technical excellence and community engagement."

Our name reflects our roots - like the gum trees that thrive in swampy conditions, we build resilient software that stands the test of time. Our location in South Carolina's coastal lowlands gives us a unique perspective on problem-solving and sustainability.
